About

I am an Associate Professor of Economics in the Department of Economics at the Sam M. Walton College of Business, University of Arkansas, where I have served on the faculty since 2008. I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from Texas A&M University.

My research develops and applies econometric methods, particularly nonparametric and time series techniques, to questions in macroeconomics, international trade, and applied economics. My work has appeared in the Journal of Econometrics, Journal of Development Economics, Econometric Reviews, and Journal of Economic Behavior and Organization, among other journals.

I teach econometrics, forecasting, and international trade at the undergraduate and graduate levels, along with Economic Analytics Powered by AI. Before entering academia, I worked in investment banking and research at Bank of China International.
